# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We expect everyone participating in WebCraft Pro to maintain a respectful, professional and constructive environment.

## Expected behavior

- Treat contributors respectfully.
- Provide constructive technical feedback.
- Focus discussions on ideas and implementation.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Accept reasonable review feedback.
- Give appropriate credit to original authors.
- Respect project licenses and attribution requirements.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions.
- Plagiarism or misrepresentation of contributions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for addressing reported violations.

Depending on severity, actions may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions or exclusion from the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers.
